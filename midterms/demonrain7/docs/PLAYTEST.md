## Invite a peer to test Catacomb II

Use the tagged Classic build with original rules. Record the tag/commit,
platform, input method and whether play is native or browser-based. Browser
play uses stepped input and has no audio. Keep debug mode off; do not expose the
developer panel or precomputed winning route. No Cloud credentials are needed
for a human to play the original game.

Read this consent script before starting:

> I am testing my course reconstruction of Catacomb II. I will take anonymous
> notes about controls, decisions, confusing moments and bugs for course
> assessment. The game includes pixel-art combat. You may pause or stop at any
> time. I will not record your voice, face or identifying details. Is that okay?

Record agreement or cancellation. Obtain separate explicit permission before
any player recording, and follow the syllabus retention requirements. Never
upload personal details to a model. The player may use larger browser zoom,
touch buttons, or pauses as needed; record accommodations affecting timing.

### Fifteen-minute session

1. **Minutes 0–2:** explain movement, holding/releasing fire, facing/strafe,
   potions, bolts and nukes. Ask the player to describe the goal in their own words.
2. **Minutes 2–10:** let them explore level 1. Observe before helping. Record
   actions, visible results, requests for help, and any reproducible issue.
3. **Minutes 10–13:** retry one confusing interaction or apparent bug from the
   same starting condition. Distinguish control misunderstanding from rule failure.
4. **Minutes 13–15:** ask what felt clear, what was confusing, when they felt in
   control, and which single change would most improve the experience.

Hypotheses: the charge/release distinction is discoverable; keyboard/touch
movement does not stick; consumables visibly affect inventory; pause/resume
preserves control; an original exit ends the episode clearly. These are
questions to test, not established findings.
